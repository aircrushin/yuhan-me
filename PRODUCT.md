# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors are potential collaborators, clients, employers, and technically curious peers who arrive from GitHub, social links, or shared projects. They want to understand taste, judgment, craft level, and whether aircrushin can turn small ideas into well-made software.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents aircrushin as an independent developer building thoughtful, opinionated tools on the web. Success means the visitor quickly understands the work, senses a high craft bar, and has a clear path into projects, writing, or contact.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, cultivated. The site should feel like a small software atelier: technical without cosplay, personal without being casual, premium without looking like a luxury template.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, oversized stat blocks, neon hacker aesthetics, glassy blur-heavy cards, identical icon grids, and portfolio pages that feel like a theme preview. Do not let the design collapse into beige editorial minimalism or dark-blue developer dashboard convention.

## Design Principles

1. Let the work feel curated, not dumped from an API.
2. Use restraint as confidence: every visual accent must carry a role.
3. Keep the human signal visible through specific copy, location, and contact paths.
4. Make technical depth legible through structure and data, not gimmicks.
5. Prefer a memorable first viewport over a generic hero stack.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, bilingual English and Chinese content, and layouts that preserve hierarchy on narrow screens.
